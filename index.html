01 / About

Research & background

I’m reading Mathematics and Computer Science at St Catherine’s College, University of Oxford, as an Ellison Scholar at the Ellison Institute of Technology. My research interests sit at the intersection of stochasticity and algorithmic optimization under uncertainty — the study of algorithms that must make good decisions with incomplete, noisy, or adversarial information.

I came to Oxford after graduating valedictorian from Quantum College, Yerevan, with a 43/45 in the IB Diploma Programme. Along the way I’ve represented Armenia at the International Olympiad in Informatics, mentored the next generation at EJOI training camps, and built production-adjacent embedded systems with the Geetak Robotics team.

“I enjoy problems where the answer isn’t just correct, but correct-enough-under-uncertainty — where the constraint is as much the world as it is the algorithm.”
